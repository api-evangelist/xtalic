# Xtalic

> Xtalic Corporation is a materials technology company founded in 2005 out of MIT by professor Chris Schuh and Dr. Alan Lund, headquartered in Marlborough, Massachusetts. Its patented computational materials design platform tailors nanostructured metal alloys — XTRONIC (nickel), LUNA (silver), XTALIUM (aluminum), PALLEX (palladium), XENERGY (soft magnetic), and XEFORM (nickel-gold) — as high-performance, sustainable replacements for traditional metals and precious-metal plating in electronics connectors, EV powertrains and charging, wireless charging, and wearables.
